==================================================
           Bruno Plus - macOS Installation
==================================================

1. INSTALLATION:
   Drag "Bruno Plus.app" into the "Applications" folder.

2. FIRST LAUNCH:
   Open your Applications folder:
   • Right-click (or Control-click) "Bruno Plus.app"
   • Select "Open"
   • Click "Open" in the dialog.

3. IF MACOS SAYS THE APP IS "DAMAGED":
   This occurs because the build is not notarized with
   an Apple Developer ID certificate (Gatekeeper quarantine).

   To fix it, open Terminal and run:
      xattr -cr "/Applications/Bruno Plus.app"

   Then launch Bruno Plus from your Applications folder.
